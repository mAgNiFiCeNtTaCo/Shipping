How do I get the file?
  1. Open the TCGplayer Seller Portal and go to Orders.
  2. Tick the orders you are shipping.
  3. Click Export Shipping. That saves a .csv file: drop it here.

The Packing Slip button makes a PDF, not this file. You can add that PDF too (step 3) to put the item lists on your slips.

Leave a blank line between addresses, or put one address per line with commas: Name, Street, City, ST ZIP.

Pasted orders print the same way, but can't go in the TCGplayer import file.
